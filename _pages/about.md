I am a fifth-year CS Ph.D. student at Stanford University. I am a member of the [Stanford Pervasive Parallelism Lab](https://ppl.stanford.edu/), and my advisor is Kunle Olukotun. I have also worked with James Zou, Junchen Jiang, and Muhammad Shahbaz. I received my Bachelor's degree from the University of Chicago, with three majors in computer science, mathematics, and statistics.

**I build AI systems that learn from experience and improve over time**, a topic known as recursive self-improvement. Examples include
[Caravan [OSDI 2024]](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi24/presentation/zhang-qizheng/),
[Agentic Plan Caching [NeurIPS 2025]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.14852),
[Agentic Context Engineering (ACE) [ICLR 2026]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04618),
[Meta-Harness [COLM 2026]](https://yoonholee.com/meta-harness/), and
[Stateless Language Agents (SLA) [preprint]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07625).
My interests span systems and frameworks, algorithms, and evaluation. Earlier, I worked on LLM inference infrastructure as part of the [LMCache](https://lmcache.ai/) project, e.g. [CacheGen [SIGCOMM 2024]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.07240) and [CacheBlend [EuroSys 2025 Best Paper]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.16444).

I have worked closely with industry partners such as SambaNova Systems, Tensormesh, Eigent AI, and Microsoft Research. My research is also used in production: ACE is deployed at [Anyshift](https://www.anyshift.io/blog/ace-in-production) and [SCX.ai](https://labs.scx.ai/ace).

In a previous life, I worked on computer networking and operating systems. I had a lot of fun hacking OS kernels and building low-latency network transport for video streaming.

My first name (Qizheng) sounds very close to "keygen" in public key encryption.
